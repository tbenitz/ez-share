Connection settings

EZShare works out of the box with PeerJS Cloud. Advanced users can enter their own secure PeerServer or TURN credentials to improve reliability.

TURN credentials entered here are stored in this browser's localStorage. Avoid long-lived privileged secrets. Changing network settings affects new sessions.
